HOUSE STYLE PROMPT BLOCK
========================
Reconciled from BRIEF.md (Saudi interactive-experiences studio: kinetic
walls, games, kinetic installations, screens with AI mapping, immersive
rooms, 24/7 operations) over the planning brief's "luxury bespoke
interior" framing where the two disagreed.

Editorial architectural photography in the language of Iwan Baan,
Hélène Binet, and Fernando Guerra, with Wallpaper-magazine staging.
Shot on a medium-format Hasselblad H6D-100c with a 35mm tilt-shift
lens equivalent. ARRI Alexa 65 acceptable for installation interiors
where the wider sensor matters. Tungsten warmth keyed to 3200K mixed
with ambient daylight at 5600K bouncing from a single tall window
camera-left. Camera height 1.55m, level horizon, no Dutch tilt,
parallel verticals.

Color grade: low-contrast filmic. Blacks lifted to charcoal #1c1c1c.
Highlights rolled to bone #f3ede2. Midtones biased warm-neutral.
Brand accent magenta (#FF5A9E or #C73D7E) appears as a discrete
single-line glow or beacon — never as wash, never as gradient,
never as overlay text. Pastel atmospheric haze (mint #5FE99A,
sky #5FA9F0, lavender #9F86E0, pink #FFD4E2, cream #FFF5E6)
appears only as ambient room tone, never as dominant subject.

Materials palette (use freely, mixed): champagne-bronze metal,
brushed steel, polished basalt, bookmatched cream travertine,
hand-rubbed brass, smoked walnut, raw oak, matte concrete, woven
linen, lime plaster. LED edge-lighting and luminous magenta light
columns ARE allowed (they are core to the brand's installations);
avoid neon tube shapes and visible cable runs.

Aspect ratio 16:9. Resolution 1928x1072 desktop / 1280x720 mobile.
Seed locked per project (see packages/content/src/seeds.ts). When
generating any subsequent frame for a project (walk start, walk end,
bridge frames touching it), pass the canonical hero JPG as a
reference image so identity is preserved.

NO PEOPLE. NO TEXT, NO SIGNAGE, NO LOGOS, NO BRAND NAMES.
NO em-dashes anywhere visible in the image.

For walk-start frames, describe ONLY the camera delta and the
absence delta from the hero ("same scene, same camera angle, same
lighting, ONLY change: remove the kinetic wall, leave the wall
plain matte cream"). Do NOT redescribe the room.
