# Basma Tech voice guide

This is the single voice that authors all copy on both sites. Lifted from `TWO-SITES-PLAN.md` section 7.

## Constraints (non-negotiable)

1. **Confident, not arrogant.** Declarative. No hedging. No "we believe".
2. **Specific over generic.** "300 stepper motors per wall." Never "many components". Name technologies (NEMA 17, Three.js, custom PCB), specific venues (Riyadh corporate atriums, AlUla cultural centers).
3. **Active voice always.** "We build" not "Our walls are built".
4. **Sentence rhythm:** short — medium — short. No paragraph of four-plus long sentences.
5. **Editorial register.** Wallpaper / Monocle / Vogue Arabia. Mature. No exclamation marks. No emojis.

## Punctuation

- ZERO em-dashes (—). Use periods, commas, colons, or restructure.
- No en-dashes (–) either.
- Arabic uses periods (.) and commas (،).

## Bilingual rule

Arabic and English are PAIRED MEANINGS, not literal translations. The Arabic version is written by an Arabic native; the English by a native English writer; both look at the same brief.

## Brand truth

- Tagline: "نهندس الدهشة" / "We Engineer Wonder"
- Metaphor: بصمة (fingerprint). Every space deserves a unique identity.
- Six capabilities: Interactive Walls, Interactive Games, Kinetic Installations, Digital Displays + AI Mapping, Immersive Environments, Operations & Management
- Process: 7 stages — Design, Program, Electronics, Fabricate, Build, Install, Operate
- Saudi-first. Vision 2030 cited only when load-bearing.
- Owner: Mohammed Anabi
- Parent: Nordbuild (Saudi)

## Structural rules

1. Cohesion across sections — each section's last sentence should bridge naturally into the next section.
2. Cohesion across pages — homepage to services to about read like one writer.
3. Cohesion across sites — Site 1 (company) is strategic, story-led. Site 2 (portfolio) is sensory, direct.
4. Headlines: max 6 words.
5. Subheads: max 12 words.
6. Body: max 3 sentences per paragraph.
7. Eyebrows: 2–4 words, monospace caps.
8. CTAs: 2–4 words, action verb first.

## Audit checklist

After every batch:

- [ ] Banned phrases? Remove.
- [ ] Em-dashes? Replace.
- [ ] Repetition across sections? Vary.
- [ ] Generic claims? Make Basma-specific.
- [ ] AR and EN paired in spirit? Adjust.
- [ ] Last sentence bridges to next? If not, rewrite.
- [ ] Reads aloud as one writer? Yes.

If any check fails, regenerate that section.
